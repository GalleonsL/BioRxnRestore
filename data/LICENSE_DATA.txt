Rhea release 139 reactions were filtered for approved, non-transport, concrete,
element/charge-balanced records; same-species participants were cancelled.
Main-species skeletons, missing-species patterns, multi-product parent
partitions, RDKit difference fingerprints and atom-mapped restoration templates
were derived with BioRxnRestore. The chemical-form snapshot files are unchanged.
These assets reuse the saved Rhea preprocessing results from September 2026;
this packaging operation does not regenerate mappings or templates.
Source IDs, version information and file checksums are retained in the files
and manifest.json. Please credit Rhea, ChEBI, RetroRules and BioRxnRestore, link
CC BY 4.0, and indicate any further modifications when redistributing.
